Common questions

Where do I find my expense system's receipt email address?

SAP Concur uses receipts@concur.com for Available Receipts. Some companies also have ExpenseIt at receipts@expenseit.com. Regional addresses exist. Workday addresses are specific to your company, so ask your expense administrator or check Workday help. If your company has not enabled email receipts, or your expense app has no receipt email, choose "Workday app, Expensify, or similar (no email)" in SlipSend.

My company uses Workday Quick Expense, which has no receipt email. What do I do?

Choose the no-email option when you set up sending. Open a trip and tap Send. Save the receipt images to your photos (or share the PDFs), tap Copy details on each receipt to paste the merchant, date, and amount, then attach the images in the Workday app. When you are done, tap "I've added these" so SlipSend marks them as handled.

How does the mileage log work?

Open Mileage under Capture, Tools, or from a trip. Add a drive with start and end odometer readings (and an optional photo of each), or type the miles. On iPhone you can also calculate the driving distance between two addresses. Share the log as a PDF and a spreadsheet, or switch on "Attach the mileage log" when you send a trip.

My receipts were sent but nothing shows up.

Your expense system only accepts mail from addresses it knows. In SAP Concur, verify the email you send from under Profile, Email Addresses. In Workday, make sure it is listed in your Contact Information. Then send again from that account.

Does SlipSend need an internet connection?

Only to send email, and for the optional city and address suggestions (and, on iPhone, driving distance). Scanning, text recognition, trips, mileage by odometer, reminders, and exports all work in airplane mode. You can turn suggestions off in Settings.

How do I back up my data?

In Settings, choose Back up data. SlipSend creates one file, with your receipts, trips, and mileage, that you save wherever you choose. To restore, choose Restore from backup on the new device.

I deleted the app. Can you recover my receipts?

No. We never have your data. Back up before deleting the app.
