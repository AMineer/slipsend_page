Capture it. Send it. Done.

Scan receipts. Organize trips. Send to your expense system.

SlipSend is a receipt app for business travelers. Snap a receipt, tag who you ate with and why, and send clean one-receipt-per-page PDFs to your expense system. Log your mileage with odometer photos. It works with SAP Concur and Workday email receipts, and with expense apps that have no receipt email.

What it does

Scan or import

Use the document scanner, or import a photo, a screenshot, or a PDF of a rideshare or web receipt. Amounts, dates, and merchants are prefilled for you to confirm.

Organize by trip

Pick a trip's start and end dates from one calendar. Receipts land in the right trip by date. Meals get attendees, a business purpose, and a tip in a few taps, with a warning when the per-person amount is over your limit.

Send in one step

One PDF per receipt goes to your expense email address from the mail account you choose, with a combined trip PDF and CSV if you want them.

No receipt email? No problem

Some expense apps, such as Workday Quick Expense, do not take email. SlipSend saves clean receipt images to your photos, shares the PDFs, and copies the merchant, date, and amount so you can add them yourself.

Mileage log

Log a drive with start and end odometer readings and a photo of each, or type the miles. On iPhone you can also get the driving distance between two addresses. Share it as a PDF and a spreadsheet, or attach it to the same email as your receipts.

Never lose a receipt

Missing a receipt right now? Log the expense, set a reminder, and attach it later. A home screen widget shows what is still unsubmitted.

Ask SlipSend

Ask plain questions about your own receipts, like what is still unsubmitted or what you spent on meals this month. Answers come from the data on your phone.

Private by design

No account. No sign-in. Your receipts, trips, and mileage stay on your device. The app does not use analytics or advertising. The only things that leave your phone are the email you choose to send and, if you leave them on, city and address suggestions from your phone's map service. Read the privacy policy.

Free, with an optional tip

SlipSend is free. If it saves you time, an optional tip helps keep it going. Tips do not change anything in the app.

Built for everyone

Large text, VoiceOver and TalkBack, light, dark, and high contrast themes, and an entry path that never requires the camera. Works on phones, tablets, and foldables.
