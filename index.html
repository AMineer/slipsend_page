Scan receipts. Organize trips. Send to your expense system.

SlipSend is a receipt app for business travelers. Snap a receipt, tag who you ate with and why, and send clean one-receipt-per-page PDFs to your expense system.

It works with SAP Concur and Workday Expenses email receipts.

Questions or early access

What it does

Scan or import

Use the document scanner, or import a screenshot or PDF of a rideshare or web receipt. Amounts, dates, and merchants are prefilled for you to confirm.

Organize by trip

Receipts land in the right trip by date. Meals get attendees and a business purpose in two taps.

Send in one step

One PDF per receipt goes to your expense email address from the mail account you choose.

Never lose a receipt

Missing a receipt right now? Log the expense, set a reminder, and attach it later. A home screen widget shows what is still unsubmitted.

Private by design

No account. No sign-in. Your receipts stay on your device. The app does not use analytics or advertising. Read the privacy policy.

Built for everyone

Large text, VoiceOver and TalkBack, light, dark, and high contrast themes, and an entry path that never requires the camera.
