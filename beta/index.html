What to try

  • Scan or import a receipt, check the details, and save it.
  • Create a trip, and pick its start and end dates from one calendar.
  • Log a drive in the mileage log, with odometer readings and photos.
  • Send a trip to your own email and open the PDFs.
  • Back up your data, then restore it.
  • If you use large text or a screen reader, walk through a few screens.

Send feedback

On iPhone, use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight, which includes a screenshot. On either platform, email support@a2m4systems.com with your device model, the screen you were on, what you did, and what happened. Please do not send real receipt images unless we ask.

Your data

Beta builds work like the real app: your receipts, trips, and mileage stay on your device. TestFlight and Google Play may share crash and usage reports with us under their own terms. Read the privacy policy.
