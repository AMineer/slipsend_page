Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026. Last updated October 20, 2026, for place suggestions, driving distance, mileage, and saving images to your photos.

The short version

SlipSend stores your data on your device. We do not run a server that receives your receipts, trips, or expenses. We cannot see them.

What stays on your device

  • Trips, expenses, mileage logs and odometer photos, attendees, notes, and settings.
  • Receipt images and the PDFs the app creates.
  • Text recognized from receipts, which is processed on your device.

What leaves your device, and only when you choose

  • Email. When you send receipts, the app opens your mail app. The message goes from your mail account to the address you configured. Your mail provider and your expense system handle it under their own policies.
  • Optional tips. SlipSend is free. If you leave a tip, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own policies. We receive no payment details and no identity from the purchase, and tips unlock nothing.
  • Place suggestions and driving distance. When you type a destination or an address, SlipSend asks the phone's map service for suggestions (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google on Android). The text you type in those fields is sent to Apple or Google, under their policies, to get suggestions. On iPhone, if you use Calculate driving distance, the two addresses are sent to Apple Maps to find the route. The Android app does not look up routes; you enter odometer readings or miles yourself. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent in the background. You can turn suggestions off in Settings, and you can skip these features by typing miles yourself.
  • Android document scanner. Scanning on Android uses a scanner module from Google Play services. Google may download that module to your phone the first time you scan. The images are processed on your device.
  • Export and backup. Files go wherever you send them through the system share sheet.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data. We do not have it.

Permissions

  • Camera, to scan receipts and to photograph an odometer. You can use manual entry, import, or the photo picker instead.
  • Photos (iPhone), add-only, and only when you tap Save images to Photos. SlipSend cannot read your photo library. On Android, saved images go to a SlipSend folder and no permission is needed.
  • Notifications, for local reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Deleting your data

Delete a receipt or trip in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. We cannot recover deleted data. Back up first if you want a copy.

Children

SlipSend is a business tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version is posted here.
